The real value of AI starts when it becomes a stable part of the work loop.

Useful positions for AI include:

- first-pass breakdowns
- rough structure drafts
- repetitive writing acceleration
- execution checklists before delivery

The point is not just to “try AI once”. The point is to place it where it repeatedly reduces cognitive load.
