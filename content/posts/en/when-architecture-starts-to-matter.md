Architecture matters when:

- new changes start affecting multiple unrelated parts
- onboarding needs too much verbal explanation
- failures become hard to localize
- long-term maintenance costs begin to dominate quick wins

Architecture is not about sounding advanced. It is about giving change a boundary.
