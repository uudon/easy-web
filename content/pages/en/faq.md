## Why use VitePress instead of a custom site?

Because the real need here is not a custom framework. It is a stable content system with clean navigation, static delivery, and low maintenance cost.

## Why keep both Chinese and English?

The Chinese site is still the primary version, but the English structure now mirrors the main site so language switching no longer falls into missing routes.
