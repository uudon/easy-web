I am Shixing.

This site is not meant to be a pile of notes. It is a long-term place to keep the parts of real work that are worth preserving:

- how AI enters practical workflows
- how programming problems get broken down and shipped
- how algorithm study becomes a reusable method
- how architectural decisions shape long-term maintenance
- how project management affects delivery quality

## What this site tries to do

I care more about these questions than polished slogans:

- why a problem is split in a certain way
- why one solution is chosen over another
- why a launch goes wrong
- why a retrospective is worth saving

If a piece of writing does not help someone work more clearly and more steadily, it probably does not deserve to stay here.
